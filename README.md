A Tableau-based exploratory data analysis project answering specific business questions about store performance, revenue trends, and customer behavior across the Rossmann retail chain.

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Rossmann store sales data using Tableau to answer a defined set of business questions — top-performing stores, sales seasonality, and customer behavior around promotions.

The dataset covers 1,017,209 records across 1,115 stores, spanning January 2013 to July 2015.

The analysis is structured as 6 focused Tableau worksheets, each built to answer one specific business question.

🎯 Project Objectives

Plot average sales trend by quarter across all years
Identify the top 10 performing stores by revenue (tree map)
Identify historical sales patterns across Rossmann stores
Identify the least-performing store by sales
Determine customer visits to Store ID 262 in Q3 2013
Compare median customer visits on promotion days vs. non-promotion days
Build a summary dashboard combining all findings, including a region-wise sales bar graph

🛠️ Technologies Used

📊 Tableau

Data visualization, tree maps, bar/line charts, dashboard design

📁 Dataset

📊 1,017,209 sales records
🏬 1,115 stores
📅 Date range: January 2013 – July 2015 (Rossmann Store Sales dataset)
🔑 Fields: Store, DayOfWeek, Date, Sales, Customers, Open, Promo, StateHoliday, SchoolHoliday

📈 Analysis Worksheets

Six worksheets, each answering one business question:

Sum of Sales, Avg of Sales & Median of Sales — overall sales distribution
Sum of Sales, Avg of Sales & Median of Sales By Month — quarterly/monthly seasonality
Top 10 Year Wise Revenue — top-performing stores by revenue (tree map)
Least Revenue Store — lowest-performing store
Customers Visited Store ID 262 in Q3 2013 — targeted customer-visit lookup
Median Value For No. of Customers Based on Promo or No Promo — promo-day behavior comparison

🔍 Key Findings

Seasonality: Sales peak every December across all years — a consistent annual pattern
Least-performing store: The lowest-revenue store trails consistently across years 
Top 10 stores by revenue:
Store 262, Q3 2013 customer visits
Median customers — Promo vs. No Promo

💡 Key Business Insights

December's consistent sales peak suggests strong, predictable holiday-season demand worth planning inventory and staffing around
